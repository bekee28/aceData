Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to pay for the consultation?
No. The Discovery consultation is completely free and never requires a card. You only pay if you decide to move forward with a Build or Build Pro engagement.
What payment methods do you accept?
Payments are handled securely by Stripe, so any major credit or debit card works. Your card details never touch our servers.
Will I get a receipt?
Yes. As soon as your payment completes you'll receive a confirmation email, and the purchase appears in your account's purchase history.
What's the difference between Build and Build Pro?
Both include the consultation. Build covers a focused 1-hour implementation; Build Pro includes 3 hours for larger or multi-step work, plus priority scheduling.
What if my project needs more than Build Pro?
No problem, many projects do. Reach out and we'll scope a custom engagement sized to your needs, applying everything we cover in the consultation.
Do I own what you build?
Yes. Everything we build is handed over to you and documented so your team can run and extend it. No lock-in.
