Services

Everything you need to put your data to work

Four core services that pivot your business to the next level.

Data warehouse architecture

A well-designed warehouse is the difference between data you can trust and data you argue about. We design and build a single source of truth, modelled for the way your business actually asks questions.

  • Schema and data modelling (dimensional / star schemas)
  • Tailored data warehouse setup
  • Clear, documented structure your team can build on

Data pipelines

Get the right data to the right place, reliably. We build ingestion and transformation pipelines that move data from your sources into your warehouse, clean, tested, and dependable.

  • ETL / ELT pipelines from APIs, databases, and files
  • Transformations and data-quality checks
  • Scheduling, monitoring, and alerting

Automated dataflows

If someone on your team is copying numbers between spreadsheets every week, that's time and accuracy you're losing. We automate the repetitive flows so reports, syncs, and updates just happen.

  • Automated reporting and dashboards
  • System-to-system syncs and integrations
  • Hands-off scheduled jobs that run reliably

AI integration

AI is most valuable when it's wired into the work you already do. We help you apply it where it genuinely saves time or surfaces insight, grounded in your own data, not hype.

  • LLM-powered assistants over your own data (RAG)
  • Automated classification, extraction, and summarization
  • Practical, measurable use cases (no science projects)
